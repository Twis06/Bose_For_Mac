# EQ presets

Start with one preset, then adjust to taste. All values are **Bose slider steps**, in the order **bass / mid / treble**. They are not parametric filters, exact dB corrections or an AutoEQ/Harman target. These listening preferences were collected for the original QC Ultra headphones; results can differ with fit, music, volume and Immersive Audio.

| Preset | Bass | Mid | Treble | What to try it for | Source |
| :-- | --: | --: | --: | :-- | :-- |
| Flat | 0 | 0 | 0 | Return to the unadjusted Bose EQ | Zeroed controls |
| Vocal clarity | −6 | +4 | +2 | Pop and vocals, with less bass emphasis | [RecordingNow V1](https://recordingnow.com/blog/bose-quietcomfort-ultra-review/) |
| Fuller detail | −4 | +5 | +3 | A fuller alternative for rock and metal | [RecordingNow V2](https://recordingnow.com/blog/bose-quietcomfort-ultra-review/) |
| Everyday balance | −2 | 0 | +3 | A modest bass cut with brighter highs | [Tom’s Guide](https://www.tomsguide.com/audio/headphones/here-are-the-3-settings-you-should-change-for-every-pair-of-bose-headphones) |
| Warm & soft | +1 | 0 | −2 | A little warmth and less treble | Bose Bar starting point |
| Speech | −3 | +3 | 0 | Try foregrounding voices in podcasts | Bose Bar starting point |

The two RecordingNow presets reproduce the reviewer's published values. Everyday balance uses Tom’s Guide's stated over-ear preference (−2 bass, unchanged mids, +3 treble); the same article suggests +2 treble as a gentler alternative. The remaining starting points are authored for this project and are not attributed to Bose or a measurement lab. Sources checked October 24, 2026. Inclusion is not endorsement by the source authors.

## Using a preset

1. Open **Equalizer** from the headphone menu.
2. Select a preset. Its numbers are visible before you click; hover for a short description and attribution.
3. The app writes the three bands sequentially and reads them back. The checkmark follows the actual headphone values, not just the last clicked name.
4. Fine-tune with the sliders. The menu shows **Custom** when no preset matches.
5. **Undo last preset** restores the snapshot taken before the most recently successful preset. The snapshot is session-only and cleared when switching or disconnecting headphones.

A three-band change is not atomic. If a band write or readback fails, Bose Bar attempts to restore and verify the previous EQ. If the connection is lost, it tells you to reconnect and inspect the values instead of claiming success. Settings live on the headphones and may also affect listening from another paired device. No preset is applied automatically on launch.
